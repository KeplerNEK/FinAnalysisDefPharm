# FinAnalysisDefPharm
Program that takes live data from trading platforms, top 4 companie of the pharmaceutical industry and the top 4 companies of the defense industry and asses their returns and volatility, which would also show risk metrics in investment. 

You will see the volatility and Sharpe of these 4 individual stocks/sector and then the average for each sector, which will then be used for comparison. 
High volatility is good with a positive sharpe.
High volatility with a negative Sharpe could indicate bad return on investment.
